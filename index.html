Featured
research

A molecular neuroscience project that introduced me to the intersection of protein interactions, disease mechanisms and the biology of the nervous system.

Fragile X-associated biology

Investigating FMRP–Orb2 colocalization through domain-specific mutations

Investigated how mutations in selected FMRP domains influence FMRP–Orb2 colocalization and subcellular distribution. The project involved generating mutant constructs, molecular cloning, transfection and confocal microscopy to examine protein localization and interaction patterns.

Research question
How do specific protein domains contribute to FMRP–Orb2 colocalization?
